# Product requirements — Missing Bulb website

The human-reviewed sink. Research from the wikis in this tree arrives here only
by a human's hand; no unattended pass edits this folder. This is the only
`product-wiki/` content the website's own code and content may build on.

The owner's stated intent below gives the growth passes their frame; the
Requirements section holds the findings the owner has since promoted.

## Stated intent (owner, at adoption)

- **The product** — a static commercial website for Missing Bulb. It boasts
  about Missing Bulb's projects, all of which live in GitHub. Missing Bulb also
  does software, management, and AI consulting, including AI Software Management
  consulting.
- **The audience** — people interested in the services of a software consultancy
  firm, in AI, and in the software development life cycle; plus people
  interested in the projects Missing Bulb builds. Deliberately wide, and the
  research is expected to stay scoped rather than survey the whole field.
- **The category** — software, management and AI consulting, including AI
  Software Management consulting. The competitor set is not yet named.

## Requirements

Promoted by the owner in session on 2026-07-30 (issue #15), distilled from the
[brand](../brand/README.md), [audience](../audience/README.md) and
[consulting-market](../consulting-market/README.md) findings:

1. **Define the category, don't assume it.** "AI Software Management" is
   Missing Bulb's coinage; the site's hero must define it in one plain
   sentence, anchored by Claudinite as the working example.
2. **Lead with Claudinite as proof of practice.** The strongest honest claim is
   dogfooding: Missing Bulb runs its own software development with AI agents
   under Claudinite (public on GitHub). Promote that over any breadth claim.
3. **Curate the portfolio rather than list it.** Feature Claudinite,
   GoogleCalendarEventCreator, TLDR and EdFringeNow with a
   what-this-demonstrates line each; collapse the rest to the org link. Frame
   the section as "working code you can read" (transparency), not adoption or
   significance the 0-star repos cannot support.
4. **Sell the engagement, not adjectives.** A "how an engagement runs" section:
   small team, hands-on building, working software in weeks, scoped
   engagements. No published pricing — the call to action is "get in touch".
5. **State experience directly; never imply it.** A who-we-are section names
   founder Ariel Raunstien with facts from his public profile (roles at Google
   and Google for Startups Israel, and Paidy; a CS degree), linking his
   LinkedIn. No invented credentials; the site's claims must match what a
   visitor finds when they check.
6. **A real contact channel:** contact@missingbulb.com.
7. **Exclusions.** No market-size figures, no testimonials or case studies
   until a real one exists, no breadth-of-engineering claims beyond what the
   portfolio (web and browser-extension work) shows.
