## Category as stated

Missing Bulb places itself in software consulting, management consulting, and AI
consulting — plus the intersection it calls AI Software Management consulting.
At adoption the owner explicitly left the competitor set unnamed, which makes
naming it the single highest-value thing a growth pass can do here: the standard
scopes competitor research against a set, and there is no set.

The three named practices are conventionally sold by very different firms —
independent software shops, management consultancies, and newer AI-specialist
outfits — so "who competes with Missing Bulb" probably has a different answer per
practice rather than one list. Confirming or refuting that shapes how the site
presents the offering: one blended pitch, or three.
