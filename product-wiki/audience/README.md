# Audience — who the site is written for

The segments the Missing Bulb website has to serve, and what each one needs from
it. Seeded, not yet researched.

## Key insights

- The audience is deliberately wide: consulting buyers, AI-curious readers, SDLC practitioners, portfolio browsers.
- The owner set an explicit research constraint: stay scoped, do not survey the whole field.
- Load-bearing unknown: which segment actually converts, and which is only traffic.

## Segments as stated

The owner named, in one breath: people interested in the services of a software
consultancy firm, people interested in AI, people interested in the software
development life cycle, and people interested in the projects Missing Bulb
builds. They flagged the breadth themselves — "it's hard to define because the
audience is wide" — with the instruction not to research the entire world.

That constraint is a scoping rule for this page, not a caveat on it. Growth
passes here should deepen one segment at a time against a concrete question from
the backlog below, rather than attempt a market-wide audience survey.

The four groups are not equivalent. Two of them (consultancy-service buyers,
SDLC practitioners) plausibly hire; two (AI-curious readers, portfolio browsers)
plausibly read and leave. Whether that split is real is the first thing worth
finding out, because the site's structure follows from it.

## Sources

No sources yet. The segments above are the owner's own description, recorded
verbatim at adoption on 2026-07-30. No audience research has been done, so
nothing here is citable and nothing should be treated as validated.

## Open questions

- Of the four stated groups, which actually contact a consultancy — and what do they search for on the way?
- What does a consulting buyer want to see in the first screen: services, proof, or people?
- Do the AI-curious and SDLC-practitioner readers convert indirectly (referrals, reputation) or not at all?
- Does the GitHub portfolio attract buyers, or mostly other developers? This decides how prominent it should be.
- What is a realistic scope boundary for audience research here, so passes stay narrow as the owner asked?
