# Brand — how Missing Bulb positions itself

What the Missing Bulb brand claims to be, and what the commercial website has to
convey for a first-time visitor to believe it. Seeded, not yet researched.

## Key insights

- Missing Bulb sells two things at once: consulting services, and a public GitHub portfolio that proves them.
- The claimed differentiator is a named category — "AI Software Management" consulting — not a researched one.
- No positioning research exists yet; every line on this page is owner-stated intent, not a finding.

## Positioning as stated

Missing Bulb does software, management, and AI consulting, and describes "AI
Software Management consulting" as its own offering. Its projects are all public
on GitHub, and the website's job is to boast about them — so the portfolio is
evidence for the consulting pitch rather than a separate section of the site.

That is the owner's framing recorded verbatim at adoption, and it is the whole
of what this page currently knows. Whether the framing lands with a buyer, and
whether "AI Software Management" reads as a category or as a coinage, are open.

## Open questions

- Does "AI Software Management consulting" match any language buyers already use, or is it a coinage that needs explaining on the page?
- Which proof carries more weight for a consulting buyer: the GitHub portfolio, or named client outcomes the site does not currently have?
- What are Missing Bulb's actual credentials and history, and how much of that belongs on the site?
- Is the brand aimed at startups, mid-market, or enterprise? The positioning language changes materially with the answer.
